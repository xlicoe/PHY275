Course Description & Rhythm

Classical mechanics is the foundation upon which modern physics is built. In this course, we trace an arc from Newtonian forces to the powerful, coordinate-free geometries of Lagrange and Hamilton.

This is not just a math course. We integrate a heavy computational thread (Brunton lab rhythm) to solve problems that analytical techniques cannot touch. We study the universe not just by tracking forces, but by understanding the fundamental principle of stationary action.

The Weekly Rhythm:

  • Mon + Wed: Taylor spine. Derivations, arguments, and physical intuition.
  • Fri — The Machine Shop: Brunton's numerical toolkit applied directly to the week's theory.

The Rituals:

  • Predict-then-check: Every demo starts with a class vote. Being wrong together is the engine.
  • The Logbook: Handwritten, one page per week. Something surprising, a pretty derivation, or a failed attempt.
  • Fermi Friday: Order-of-magnitude puzzles opening each Machine Shop.
